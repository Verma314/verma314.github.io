There probably exist many heuristics for human learning and skill
acquisition. And perhaps a huge chunk of them will be obvious to the
readers.

This one is about how confusion is a good sign. It’s a necessary part of
learning, and if you’re feeling it while learning something new it probably
indicates that your brain is giving you a signal to try to fill in the
gaps.

Often we tend to run away from confusion, or take it as a sign that
whatever we are doing is not working. But perhaps, this feeling should be
celebrated. Confusion indicates you’re out of your comfort zone and need to
look for answers.

That might be the best time to pen down the exact precise questions that
are bothering you, maybe a list of questions, really drill down on them
making them precise, and then set out to searching for their answers.

TL;DR: do not run away from confusion!

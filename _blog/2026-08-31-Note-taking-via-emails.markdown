I've configured a AppScript on my Google account, so that whenever I send
an email to myself
with the subject line starting with the keyword {BLOG}, it will
automatically trigger my github blog
and push this content to the live blog. This will hopefully prove to be a
brilliant idea to capture interesting ideas and concepts on the go, or
while watching/attending lectures etc.

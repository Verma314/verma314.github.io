1. Meta-cognition:

Ability of an agent or human, to be  aware of its own  internal states, and to have algorithms (or heuristics) to predict its future states in response to various anticipated or existing stimuli.

2. Curiosity and Agency:

Abiility of an agent or human to pursue self-directed learning, knowledge, skill-acquision, or self-improvement. Also known as recursive self-improvement. 

3. Observation:

Ability of an agent or human to accept, parse, and attend to various external (or internal) inputs in the domain.  


4. Attention:

Ability of the agent or human to rank the input in decreasing order or importance.

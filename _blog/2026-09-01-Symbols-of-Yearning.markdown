I’ve often caught myself thinking in terms of the future. Yearning for
things, for places to go, for things to do, things to be and so. Nietzsche
would perhaps say they give us a glimpse of who we could be, our potential.

I am not fully sure if it’s an ideal state to be in. I think it sometimes
might be useful to think in terms of the present.

For example, reading a poem today, instead of wishing that we will read
poetry on the beach on a vacation someday gaining insights into the human
psyche; taking a photograph of the sky from your phone on an evening walk
instead of wanting to take photos.
